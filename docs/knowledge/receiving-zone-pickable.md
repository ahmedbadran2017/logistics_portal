**Measured 2026-08-27 (corrects an older belief that Receiving was settings-excluded):** prod `lp_excluded_zones` does NOT contain `Receiving Zone - JM` (nor `SLOW ZONE - JM` — SLOW is only blocked on ee's side via `get_rejected_warehouses`). Receiving is pickable on purpose: the dropship flow (per-order PO → PR into Receiving → DN out the same day, e.g. Maison good and well) ships straight from it. Do NOT "fix" this by excluding Receiving — it would break dropship picking.

**The failure mode (pillow case, item 47439676768510, order #255634):** dropship pieces received 17/08 for order #254683 → order CANCELLED 19/08 → pieces vanished physically with no document → ledger kept 2 in Receiving → the next order for the product (#255634) allocated its pick from Receiving → picker (Hamza) sent to an empty zone → short-pick pulled the order off the list (portal behaved correctly at every step; the DATA was the lie).

**Scale at discovery:** 594 items / 3,049 units parked in Receiving Zone; **545 items / 2,066 units untouched 3+ days** — a mix of put-away backlog (real stock) and phantom leftovers (cancelled dropship, [backdated-admin-recos](backdated-admin-recos.md) restatements). Receiving must behave as a pass-through.

**Guard built (`869eb82`):** problem_radar check `staleReceiving` — Bins in Receiving with qty>0 whose last SLE is 3+ days old (count + units), routes to Move Stock's put-away queue. Floor remedy: work the put-away queue; what isn't physically there gets cycle-counted to 0.

**SUPERSEDED for the veto question — measured 2026-09-30 on prod:** `Receiving Zone - JM` is **no longer pickable**. `eman@justyol.com` ticked `Warehouse.is_rejected_warehouse` 0→1 on **2026-09-29 18:23** (Version `s1hnt6ttc6`; the same flag was flipped on and off by `anaskarrassi@gmail.com` twice on 2026-04-28). The note above is still correct that Receiving is NOT in `lp_excluded_zones` — that was always the wrong place to look. The veto that matters comes from the OTHER set: `ecommerce_integrations.overrides.pick_list.get_rejected_warehouses()`, wrapped by `picking._ee_rejected()` (logistics_portal/api/picking.py:2057) and intersected into availability at :2261.

**Proven identity:** `get_rejected_warehouses() == {Warehouse where is_rejected_warehouse=1}` exactly (368 warehouses, set difference empty both ways). So **one checkbox on one Warehouse card silently vetoes picking** — no portal setting, no deploy, no audit trail the floor can see. `lp_excluded_zones` (28 zones) is a separate, smaller list.

**Blast radius of that one tick:** 714 items / 6,116 units in Receiving went unpickable overnight. Worked example: item `48981335081214` (SKU 600365544, T-shirts TSH Men-Brown / M), 1 unit received 2026-09-19 13:25 site-time by PR `MAT-PRE-2026-03698` from supplier Town Team — order `#260787` (dated 2026-09-18, one day BEFORE the goods landed) shows `pickable 0` purely because of the flag. `availability(["48981335081214"])` returns empty from prod.

**Second, independent veto on the same order:** its SO line points at `Morocco - JM`, which is in BOTH the rejected set AND `lp_excluded_zones`, and whose Bin reads `actual_qty 0 / reserved_qty 1` — a phantom hold with **zero** Stock Reservation Entry rows behind it. That phantom `reserved_qty` is the `reserve` chip the SKU lookup shows. Site-wide: **17,315 Bin rows / 158,396 units** carry `reserved_qty > 0` with no live SRE — far bigger than the 497/539u in [stale-sre-holds](stale-sre-holds.md), and it is the Bin counter that is wrong, not just the SRE rows.

**Cohort (September 2026 open lines only; the all-time number is 20,572 orders and is inflated by legacy junk):** of 3,442 open lines, **406 lines / 381 orders** have their entire free balance sitting in a vetoed zone — **55 of those orders are starved specifically by Receiving Zone**. See [availability-one-definition](availability-one-definition.md) and [measure-dont-trust-audits](measure-dont-trust-audits.md).

**WHY the checkbox keeps flipping — Receiving Zone does TWO opposite jobs (measured 2026-09-30, SLEs since 2026-08-01):** it is simultaneously a cross-dock lane (must be pickable) and a put-away buffer (must not be). Outbound: 2,450 Delivery Note rows / 2,961u shipped STRAIGHT OUT of Receiving, plus 149 Stock Entry rows / 1,935u moved to shelves. Inbound: 782 Purchase Receipt rows / 2,041u, plus 405 Stock Entry rows / **5,285u** transferred IN (people use it as overflow). Dwell is bimodal, not a spread: **36.5% leave within 2h** (cross-dock working) while **30.2% sit past 72h** (put-away backlog); median 21.5h, p90 **284h (~12 days)**. One `is_rejected_warehouse` checkbox cannot serve both, which is why it was flipped 3 times by 2 different people.

**The routing signal exists but is half-broken.** Cross-dock receipts into Receiving split: `sales_order` link filled = 305 rows/336u/275 receipts; **order number only in the PO's NAME string** (e.g. PO `#260787-Town Team-JM`, `sales_order` EMPTY) = 382 rows/401u/**351 receipts**; genuine bulk `C_plain_PO` = 96 rows/**1,303u**/54 receipts. So ~626 of 680 receipts are cross-dock by document count while bulk dominates by units — and **more than half of cross-dock carries its order only as a substring of the PO name**. Those names DO resolve: `SUBSTRING_INDEX(purchase_order,'-',1)` matched a real Sales Order **576/576 = 100%**. Same failure family as [desk-decisions-leave-no-portal-fields](desk-decisions-leave-no-portal-fields.md).

**Design conclusion (mine, not yet decided by Ahmed):** cross-dock belongs in its OWN pickable warehouse, with Receiving demoted to a non-pickable put-away buffer — routing on PO linkage, which is mechanical, not a judgment call. Fixing `sales_order` on the dropship PR is the prerequisite; until then any rule must parse the PO name.

**WHY the 2026-09-29 tick happened — Ahmed confirmed, and the ledger shows it:** a new container was moved in and was still being counted. `STE-11163` (Stock Entry, **4,413 units over 336 lines**, posted 2026-09-29 by `eman@justyol.com`) landed the container **into Receiving Zone**, and the same person ticked `is_rejected_warehouse` at 18:23 the same day to stop pickers touching uncounted stock. That is a correct instinct applied to a shared zone — the veto is blunt, not wrong.

**What it caught besides the container (measured 2026-09-30):** Receiving held 714 items / 6,116u, of which the container is 4,413u. The collateral is **1,631 units / 378 items** with nothing to do with it: consignment 1,373u/287 items, cross-dock 110u/60 items, our own older bulk 148u/31 items. Cross-tab by age is clean — "our bulk, arrived in the last 5 days" IS the container (326 items/4,413u); everything else predates it.

**The structural fix (not yet done):** the pattern already exists — `Container 11 … 30 - JM` warehouses, all permanently `is_rejected_warehouse=1`. Had STE-11163 targeted one of those, no tick would have been needed and nothing unrelated would have frozen. A container should land in its own container warehouse, be counted there, and move to shelves from there; Receiving then never has to close. Until that is the habit, every container repeats this.

**Cleanest remedy for the current state:** leave the veto alone (it is right for the container) and evacuate the 1,631u residue — consignment to their CN sections, cross-dock to `Cross-dock - JM`, old bulk to shelves or cycle-counted to zero. `stock_moves.putaway_queue` already suggests the correct target for the consignment rows since the 2026-09-30 deploy.
